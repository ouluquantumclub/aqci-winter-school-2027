ABOUT THE SCHOOL

From quantum concepts to practical computing.

The Applied Quantum Computing International Winter School will bring together an international community in Oulu to learn, discuss and work with quantum computing in a practical setting.

The programme will combine expert lectures, hands-on sessions and opportunities for discussion across quantum algorithms, software, hardware, applications and the wider quantum ecosystem.
